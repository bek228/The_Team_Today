# Self-audit of v1 (skeptical-professor review)

This is the team's own skeptical review of v1 at commit ada311a, run with the course's skeptical-review prompt. Findings only. Nothing was fixed.

Method: read `src/*.py`, `README.md` and `specs_tried.md`, and read the CSVs in `outputs/tables/`. `run_all.py` was not run. Anything not verified is marked "unverified".

## Findings, ranked by severity

| S1 | Major | The universe is picked with knowledge of the future. A 2020 member is only a candidate if Yahoo can still price it in 2026. Firms that died after 2020 (TWTR, ATVI, XLNX, FRC, SIVB) cannot enter the top 50, so the ranking is among survivors. The README says "no hindsight" in two places, but this step uses hindsight. | S2 | Major | The whole day-0 rule rests on reading Yahoo's `GradeDate` as UTC. The only evidence is the shape of the clock-time histogram (peak 04:00-08:00 ET). ## Checked and fine

- README numbers: 30 numbers traced to `outputs/tables/` and all match. Examples: `share_before_open` 0.7913 (79%), `share_near_earnings` 0.2242 (22%), `share_episodes_earnings_in_window` 0.7215 (72%), 47 of 505 members unpriced, 77 multi-broker episodes, 1,422 non-mixed episodes, 49 clusters, t = -1 and t = 0 daily ARs (+0.52 / -0.70, +1.80 / -1.74), near-earnings t = -1 (+1.58 / -2.92), no-earnings t = -1 (+0.30 / -0.13), earnings at t = -1 (+3.72 / -6.00, n = 52 / 59), during-market t = 0 (+1.22 / -1.36), after-close upgrades (+2.24, +1.93, +0.53; 12 and 4 episodes), regression p-values (0.0535, 0.0178) and coefficients (1.36, 0.53, 5.20, 2.32), rank 1 brokers (JP Morgan +4.20 n 22, B of A Securities -3.34 n 19), Spearman range 0.91-0.95, tau range 0.25-0.72, CAR[0,+1] (+1.60 / -1.23), SBUX +23.37 and AMD +23.12, Kruskal-Wallis p-values, and the results block (headline, window and equality tables) against `window_summary.csv`, `broker_equality_test.csv`. The META, C, GE and BRK-B coverage holes match `ratings_coverage_by_year.csv`.
- Merges: every `merge` in `src/04_build_event_panel.py` (lines 233, 289), `src/05_results.py` (lines 84, 86, 102), `src/00_universe.py` (line 259) and the dashboard has `validate=`. Broker aliases are asserted unique (`src/_common.py:138`).
- Estimation window [-250,-30] ends before the event window, so there is no overlap with the event itself. `assert (ev['day0'] >= ev['announce_date'])` guards day 0 (`src/04_build_event_panel.py:293`).
- Units: CARs stay as fractions until `mean_se` multiplies by 100 and the shrinkage step divides by 100 again. No mismatch found. Dates are Eastern, matched to trading days only through `find_day0`.
- No hard-coded absolute paths, keys or tokens in `src/`, `dashboard/` or `run_all.py`. Paths come from `_common.py`; `.env` and `secrets.toml` are in `.gitignore`.
- Downloads fail loudly (`SystemExit`) instead of continuing with gaps. The permutation test is seeded (331) and checked against scipy's F.
- Dropped rows are logged (`ratings_duplicates_dropped.csv`, `dropped_events.csv`). `n_rating_changes_dropped` is 0.
